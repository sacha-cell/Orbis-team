Pricing questions

Do the fees include media spend?

No. The monthly fee covers Orbis Team's strategy, planning, buying and oversight. Media spend is separate and set by your plan.

How does an engagement start?

Engagements open with a 90-day readiness phase: we review your current plan with an Orbis Audit, get data and tracking in order, and agree the one outcome the program will be judged on. Once that's in place, we move into a regular review cadence.

Which tier is right for us?

It depends on how many channels and teams the budget covers, not on company size alone. One priority channel usually fits Launch. Retail media, CTV and programmatic running together usually fits Accelerate. Several teams or brands sharing one budget usually fits Dominate. A 30-minute call is the fastest way to confirm.

Can we change tiers later?

Yes. The tier follows the scope, so it can move up or down as channels and teams are added or removed.

Do you take platform incentives or volume commitments?

No. Orbis Team has no platform quotas and no volume commitments, so recommendations are based on what's right for your plan.

Which channels and platforms do you work across?

Retail media networks, connected TV, and programmatic display, video, audio and podcast, with access through 20+ DSPs. Plans are built across channels rather than inside a single platform.
